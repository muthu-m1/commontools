    Private by design

    Common Tools runs entirely in your browser. There is no sign-up and no tracking code, so nothing you type ever leaves your device. Every tool shows its formula and, where a published standard exists, links to it, from the Department of Energy's appliance guide to the 2025 resuscitation guidelines. Automated tests check the maths against reference values. Install it once and all eighteen tools open without a connection, on a plane or in a basement with no signal. It is free to use and free to share, with nothing to sign up for. Add it to your phone.
