How this is calculated

GPA = Σ (grade points × credits) ÷ Σ credits Cumulative = (old GPA × old credits + term points) ÷ all credits Final needed = (target − now × (1 − w)) ÷ w, w = final's weight Weighted = Σ (weight × score) ÷ Σ weights

Grade points use the common US 4.0 scale: A 4.0, A− 3.7, B+ 3.3, B 3.0, B− 2.7, C+ 2.3, C 2.0, C− 1.7, D+ 1.3, D 1.0, D− 0.7, F 0. Some schools give an A+ 4.3, use whole letters only, or weight honors and AP classes; choose the option that matches your school. Pass/fail courses carry no grade points and are left out.

"Your grade now" means your average on everything except the final. If the weights you enter for categories do not add up to 100, the grade is calculated from the categories you entered, scaled to 100%.

Note

Every school publishes its own grading policy, usually in the student handbook or course syllabus. When this calculator and your school's policy differ, your school's policy wins.
