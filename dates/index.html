Date Calculator

Count days, add months, work out an exact age or a deadline in business days.

Years and months are added first, then weeks and days. Adding a month to 31 January gives the last day of February.

Counts Monday to Friday, including both the start and end dates.

The start date itself is not counted, like a spreadsheet WORKDAY function. Use a negative number to count back.

How this is calculated

Dates are treated as whole calendar days in the Gregorian calendar, with no time of day, so daylight-saving changes and time zones never shift a result.

  • Days between is the end date minus the start date. Tick "count the end date" to include both days, for example when counting the days of a trip.
  • Months and years: a month is complete when the same day of the month comes round. When that day does not exist (the 31st in a 30-day month, or 29 February in a common year), the last day of the month counts. Someone born on 29 February turns a year older on 28 February in common years by this rule; some laws use 1 March instead.
  • Business days are Monday to Friday. US federal holidays follow 5 U.S.C. 6103 with the federal employee rule: a holiday that falls on a Saturday is observed the Friday before, and on a Sunday the Monday after. Private employers and other countries have different holidays.
  • Week numbers follow ISO 8601: weeks start on Monday, and week 1 is the week with the year's first Thursday.

Sources

  • 5 U.S. Code § 6103: federal holidays
  • US Office of Personnel Management: federal holidays and observed dates
  • ISO 8601: date and time format, including week numbers
