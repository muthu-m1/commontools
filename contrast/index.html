How this is calculated

WCAG compares the relative luminance of the two colours, a measure of how bright each looks:

channel c = value ÷ 255 linear c ≤ 0.04045 ? c ÷ 12.92 : ((c + 0.055) ÷ 1.055)^2.4 L = 0.2126 R + 0.7152 G + 0.0722 B ratio = (L lighter + 0.05) ÷ (L darker + 0.05)

The ratio runs from 1:1 (no contrast) to 21:1 (black on white). It is shown cut off at two decimals, never rounded up, because WCAG treats 4.499 as a fail.

Minimums

  • AA, body text: 4.5:1 (success criterion 1.4.3).
  • AA, large text: 3:1. Large means at least 18 pt (24 px), or 14 pt (about 18.66 px) in bold.
  • AAA: 7:1 for body text and 4.5:1 for large text (1.4.6).
  • Icons, borders and form controls: 3:1 against what is next to them (1.4.11).

Logos and purely decorative text are exempt. Suggested fixes keep the hue and saturation and move only the lightness, by the smallest step that passes.
